## Why AI draft import exists

External AI often produces unstable IDs and noisy progress metadata. A human-readable draft format lets users focus on concept quality while Synapse generates strict internal graph fields consistently.

## Why IDs/progress are generated by Synapse

- Avoids ID collisions and broken references from external generation.
- Keeps edge slots unique per source card automatically.
- Ensures edge-level progress starts in a clean zeroed state.
- Reduces friction so AI outputs can remain simple and editable.

## Expected workflow (NotebookLM and similar tools)

1. Use NotebookLM (or equivalent) as a reference-analysis stage to discover missing concepts/links.
2. Use GPTs/Gems/ChatGPT/Gemini (or manual editing) to format that output into Synapse draft JSON.
3. Import draft into Synapse via **Import AI Draft**.
4. Review in Learn mode, then refine graph content iteratively.

## Explicit non-integration statement

Synapse remains provider-agnostic and local-first. It does **not** directly integrate with NotebookLM, GPTs, Gems, or backend APIs.
